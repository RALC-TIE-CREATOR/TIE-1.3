### Novedades de V1.3

- **B1 (TC5a):** `a_tr = a_lat/(2πn) = cH₀/(2π)` con `H₀ = 1/(nT₀)`; el factor `1/(2π)` es la fase de un tick, no un parámetro libre.
- **B3 (TC2):** PC4 se deriva del tick (`H₀T₀ = 1/n`); `z = e^{H₀d/c} − 1`; `d_L(z)` no tiene predicción distintiva y se declara así.
- **B2 (TC10a):** la lectura Hausdorff de los pesos topológicos es un no-go; los pesos quedan como ansatz.
- **B5/B6:** `μ(x) = x/√(1+x²)` y contraste SPARC con `a_tr` congelado.
- Frentes abiertos: equivalencia inercial-gravitatoria (C1), torsión del fotón (C2), `Gr_reposo = c²` y derivación de `m₀`.

### What is new in V1.3

- **B1 (TC5a):** `a_tr = a_lat/(2πn) = cH₀/(2π)` with `H₀ = 1/(nT₀)`; the `1/(2π)` factor is the phase of one tick, not a free parameter.
- **B3 (TC2):** PC4 is derived from the tick (`H₀T₀ = 1/n`); `z = e^{H₀d/c} − 1`; `d_L(z)` has no distinctive prediction and is declared as such.
- **B2 (TC10a):** the Hausdorff reading of the topological weights is a no-go; the weights remain an ansatz.
- **B5/B6:** `μ(x) = x/√(1+x²)` and the SPARC contrast with `a_tr` frozen.
- Open fronts: inertial-gravitational equivalence (C1), photon torsion (C2), `Gr_reposo = c²` and the derivation of `m₀`.
